A growth plan for Nykaa

A plan to turn beauty repeat shoppers into fashion customers and keep loyalty members coming back.

The starting point

Public fact: Said about 79% of its beauty GMV in Q2 FY24 came from customers who already existed on the platform when the year began (earnings call). Source

What I would test

1

Lifecycle

Replenishment reminders by product type. Skincare, haircare and makeup run out at different times, so each reminder is timed to the pack and uses the shopper’s own shade, skin or hair profile.

2

Cross-sell

Show Nykaa Fashion picks to beauty repeaters who have never bought fashion, then compare the cost of those customers with paid acquisition.

3

Loyalty

Show each Prive member how far they are from the next tier, with one reason to come back this month.

How I would measure it

North star: Repeat rate by categoryBeauty-to-fashion crossover
