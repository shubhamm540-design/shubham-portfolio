A growth plan for Myntra

A plan to make loyalty tiers visible and use wishlist triggers to bring shoppers back before a sale.

Email ShubhamLinkedIn

By Shubham Mishra, growth and lifecycle marketer. Built from public information only.

The starting point

Public fact: Its Insider loyalty programme had about 6.8 million members in 2020, who contributed over 40% of sales (Business Insider India). Source

What I would test

1

Loyalty

Show every shopper how close they are to the next Insider tier, and send a pre-sale nudge to those within reach.

2

Lifecycle

Wishlist triggers: price-drop and back-in-size alerts for saved items, since Insider rewards browsing and wishlisting as well as buying (YourStory, 2018).

3

Content

Turn stylist sessions and creator looks into shoppable looks, then compare repeat visits of people who watched with those who did not.

How I would measure it

North star: Insider share of ordersVisit frequency
