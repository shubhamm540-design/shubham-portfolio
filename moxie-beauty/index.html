GEO playbook

1 · Answer pages

Direct answer in 2 lines, then a facts table. FAQ, Product, Review schema. Engines favour extractable facts 3rd-party est. ↗

2 · Be where AI looks

Reddit + YouTube. Honest 30-day reviews; chaptered videos with transcripts 3rd-party est. ↗

3 · Third-party proof

Trichologist bylines, press, rich Nykaa/Amazon copy. Refresh top pages monthly.

4 · Technical

Bing Webmaster + Search Console, text not hidden in JS, one canonical per product.

5 · Long prompts

Write for "2C hair, Mumbai, flaky scalp, ₹500 budget" queries. Quiz returns the routine.

6 · Measure

30-prompt panel monthly across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Overviews: mentioned? position? sentiment? source?
