Executive summary

Moxie is winning on shelf and in quick commerce. The part I can't see from outside is what happens after the first order.

I spent a day inside Moxie's ad library, website, Nykaa listings and competitor ads. Three things stood out:

1 · The sale is loud, the proof is quiet.Almost half of live ads are one birthday-sale creative that has been up for a week. The ads that last (60+ days) are calm quick-commerce messages.
2 · Scalp is under-fed.Only 12 of 100 ads touch dandruff or scalp, yet Fix My Curls' founder says hair-fall and dandruff drive repeat demand.
3 · Marketplaces hold the proof.Nykaa shows 9K–14K ratings on top SKUs; the brand site shows 75–285 reviews. The reviews live where Moxie doesn't own the customer.
My take

The upside isn't more reach. It is turning a Nykaa or Zepto buyer into a WhatsApp-reachable routine buyer, and giving the scalp range the airtime the data says it has earned.

Key Findings & Opportunities

  1. Quick-commerce messaging is the most durable angle in the account; scale it into education, not just delivery.
  2. Scalp range is a proven-demand category with the thinnest ad support.
  3. All revenue, retention and run-rate figures are company claims; I flag them and never use them as inputs.

04

Positioning audit

Moxie says "clean + science". What a visitor actually sees first is "19% off".

The promise

Clean, dermatologist-input, built for waves and curls. "We spend 3–4x more on actives."

What the evidence shows

Observed46 of 100 ads and the site popup lead with the discount. Proof assets (before/after, clinical data) are rare.

The gap

My hypothesisPremium claims sold with a gift-with-purchase teach shoppers to wait for the sale.

My take

Two voices, one brand: a doctor on the scalp ads, a best friend on the sale ads. Both can work, but nothing ties them together visually.

Key Findings & Opportunities

  1. Create one repeatable proof device (a "hair-type badge" + routine name) that appears on every ad and the site.
  2. Keep the doctor voice for scalp, creator voice for curls, and make the logo/colour system the glue.

Recommendations, experiments & roadmap

Specific actions with stop/go rules.

1 · Creative system (three pillars)

Pillar A: Hair-type education

Reels 20–30s, 3/week. Hooks: "Frizzy or wavy?", "2C vs 3A in 10 seconds".

Pillar B: Scalp science

Doctor-voice Reels + carousels with stats, 2/week. Hooks: "Dandruff vs dry scalp", "% flakes after 4 washes" (only with real data).

Pillar C: 30-day proof

UGC before/after, 2/week. Hook: "Day 1 vs Day 30".

Format split: 70% video, 20% carousel, 10% static. 8 new creatives a week; offers capped at 25% of spend.

Insert card in every Nykaa, Amazon, Zepto order: QR → 3-question quiz → routine → WhatsApp opt-in with ₹100 off the next order.

Lift scalp share of ads from 12% to 30%; bundle Dandruff Detox Pre-Wash + Scalp Reviving Shampoo; 4-week routine ad.

GEO playbook (what to do, in order)

1 · Answer pages

One page per question. Open with a 2-sentence direct answer, then a table of facts (hair type, wash frequency, ingredient, %). Add FAQ, Product and Review schema. Generative engines prefer extractable facts and pages that anticipate follow-ups.

2 · Be where AI looks

Reddit and YouTube are the most cited sources in AI Overviews. Seed honest 30-day reviews, run an official brand account that answers questions on r/curlyhair-style communities, and publish chaptered YouTube explainers with full transcripts.

3 · Third-party proof

Trichologist-bylined articles, press, Nykaa and Amazon listings with hair-type and concern in the title and bullets. Perplexity favours fresher content, so refresh the top pages monthly.

4 · Technical basics

Submit sitemap to Bing Webmaster Tools and Google Search Console, make sure pages load without JavaScript blocking text, and keep one canonical URL per product.

5 · Write for long prompts

People now type 60-word prompts: "I have 2C hair in Mumbai, flaky scalp, budget ₹500, what routine?" Build pages that answer that exact shape, with a quiz that returns a routine.

6 · Measure AI share of voice

Keep a panel of 30 prompts. Every month, run them in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews and log: mentioned? position? sentiment? source cited. Tools like Semrush AIO or Otterly can automate it.

Marketplace ranking (Nykaa, Amazon, quick commerce)

My hypothesis Titles: Hair type + concern + product (for example "Moxie Scalp Reviving Shampoo for Dandruff-Prone Wavy Hair"). Add rating velocity as a goal: scalp SKUs from ~500 to 2,000+ Nykaa ratings in 90 days via post-purchase WhatsApp review asks (day 7). Seed Q&A with real answers. On quick commerce, the first two images decide the click; use "hair type + result" instead of the product shot.

My take

Ranking on Google is a list. Ranking in AI is a shortlist of two or three brands. Moxie is competing for one of those slots, and the winners will be the brands that have the clearest facts and the most credible third-party mentions.

Key Findings & Opportunities

  1. Own the hair-type and scalp question pages: they are both high-intent and under-served by brand content.
  2. Reddit and YouTube presence is a GEO lever, not just a social one.
  3. Measure AI share of voice from day one; without a baseline, you cannot show the work is paying off.
