Part 1: Brand X-Ray

Sections 1-5: brand, market, positioning, products and pricing

1. Executive summary

Moxie is a two-year-old textured-hair brand (wavy, curly, plus a fast-growing scalp range) that has gone from a Gurugram start-up to a ₹100 Cr+ run-rate claim and a $15M Series A. Its growth engine is visible from the outside: education-led creative, a heavy 19%-off promotional calendar, and distribution across Nykaa, Amazon and quick commerce.

  • Verified factFounded 2023 by Nikita Khanna and Anmol Ahlawat; $15M Series A led by Bessemer (Dec 2025).
  • Observed signalOn 22 Nov 2026 the Meta Ad Library showed 100 active India ads. 46 of them push one birthday sale (flat 19% off, free gifts) and ~21 unique copy variants carry all 100 ads, so creative variety is thin.
  • Observed signalThe site opens a phone-number capture popup for 19% off; the same discount is used for acquisition, marketplace parity and the birthday sale. Discount is the main lever.
  • HypothesisBiggest lever is not more reach: it is turning a first marketplace order into a routine (shampoo + mask + leave-in) that Moxie can reach again on WhatsApp.

My three-line thesis

  • Own the routine. A first buyer on Nykaa or Zepto rarely becomes a first-party customer; an insert card + WhatsApp opt-in can change that.
  • Own the scalp. Dandruff/hair fall is the largest repeat-demand pocket in Indian haircare and Moxie has a range but only 12 of 100 ads on it.
  • Reduce discount dependence. Replace blanket 19% with value-add (sampler, routine quiz, subscribe-and-save).

Key Findings & Opportunities

  • Moxie is ad-creative-concentrated: 100 ads, ~21 copy variants, one dominant offer.
  • Opportunity: a 3-pillar creative system (see section 14) that keeps the sale but adds always-on education and scalp.
  • Caveat: all revenue/retention figures are company claims; ad spend and ROAS are not visible and are not estimated here.

2. Brand overview

  • Verified factPositioning: "100% clean" haircare for textured hair, vegan and cruelty-free, with a "Moxie List of Nasties" of 1,400+ blacklisted ingredients (brand website).
  • Verified factFounders: Nikita Khanna and Anmol Ahlawat (earlier co-founded Instago). Raj Shamani is a strategic advisor.
  • Verified factFunding: ₹17.3 Cr seed (Fireside, Jul 2024); $15M Series A (Bessemer, Fireside, angels) on 16 Dec 2025. Total roughly $17.8M per Tracxn/CB Insights.
  • Verified factChannels: own site, Nykaa (from ~Oct 2024), Amazon, Swiggy Instamart, Blinkit, Zepto.
  • Observed signalBrand voice on site and ads: science + friendly creator tone ("Good curls, on demand"). Testimonials show age, concern and hair type, a good personalised-proof format.
  • 3rd-party estimateFY25 revenue ₹18.6 Cr per RoC filings (Inc42); company says ₹100 Cr+ run-rate in late 2025. These are not the same metric; I treat the latter as a claim.

Key Findings & Opportunities

  • Brand story is strong on ingredients and science, but the site leads with a discount rather than the story.
  • Opportunity: use founder-led and dermatologist/trichologist proof above the fold outside sale windows.

4. Positioning audit

What Moxie says

Clean, science-led, for waves and curls; scalp health; "3-4x more spend on actives".

What the evidence shows

Observed signalAds and site lean on offers (19% off, free gifts worth ₹995 / ₹2,495) more than on proof.

Gap

HypothesisClaim-led but proof-light: few ads show before/after, clinical data or ingredient demos.

  • Observed signalTwo distinct voices: creator-casual (birthday sale) and doctor-style (scalp). Brand consistency is loose.
  • HypothesisThe scalp range is positioned like a medical product but sold with a gift-with-purchase promo; mixed signals.

Key Findings & Opportunities

  • Positioning is clear for curls, less clear for scalp.
  • Opportunity: one-line brand promise per range ("Waves, defined. Scalp, balanced.") with a consistent proof device (hair-type badge).

My Growth Playbook

Sections 14-15: recommendations, experiments, roadmap and KPIs

14. Recommendations, experiments & roadmap

Recommendation 1: A three-pillar creative system

  • Pillars: hair-type education, scalp science, 30-day proof.
  • Formats: 70% 20-30s video, 20% carousel, 10% static.
  • Cadence: 8 new creatives/week, 3 per pillar plus 2 offers.
  • Example hooks: "Is it frizz or is it waves?"; "Dandruff or dry scalp? Do this 10-second test."; "I washed with Moxie for 30 days."

Recommendation 2: Marketplace-to-first-party bridge

  • Insert card in every Nykaa/Amazon/quick-commerce order: QR to a 3-question hair-type quiz and a routine, WhatsApp opt-in with a ₹100 off second order.
  • Track opt-in rate by channel.

Recommendation 3: Scalp as growth pillar

  • Raise scalp share of ads from 12% to 30%; doctor-style hook with a 4-week routine.
  • Add a Dandruff Detox Pre-Wash + Scalp Reviving Shampoo bundle.

The plan above is designed to be run in 90 days with specific tests and clear stop/go rules.
