What I would test

1

Gamification

A fandom challenge. Fans post their tee with a show, sport or meme tag, climb a leaderboard and win merch drops.

2

Lifecycle

Tag buyers by the fandom they bought into, and send the next drop in that fandom instead of a blanket sale.

3

Unit economics

A cohort view of repeat by acquisition channel, so spend shifts to channels whose buyers reorder.
