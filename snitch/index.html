A growth plan for Snitch

A plan to bring a drop-driven menswear buyer back for the next drop, in the app and in store.

Email ShubhamLinkedIn

By Shubham Mishra, growth and lifecycle marketer. Built from public information only.

The starting point

Public fact: Crossed ₹500 Cr revenue in FY25 (Inc42). Its CMO puts repeat purchase at about 38 to 42%, strongest among app users (MediaBrief). Source

What I would test

1

Lifecycle

A drop-alert journey. Tell past buyers when a new drop lands in their size and fit, by push and WhatsApp, in the weeks after delivery.

2

Loyalty

Make Snitch X tiers visible in the app, so the second order clearly moves a customer toward the next tier.

3

Omnichannel

Invite store shoppers to the app with a first-drop reward, so offline buyers become app repeaters.

How I would measure it

North star: 30 and 90-day repeat rateApp share of repeat orders
