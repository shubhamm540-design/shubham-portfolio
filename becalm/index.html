A plan to win sensitive-skin buyers through search and keep them through the first eight weeks of a routine.

The starting point

Public fact: Presents itself as calming and clinical skincare, dermatologist tested for oily and sensitive skin (Instagram bio). Source

What I would test

1

SEO

Pages for the questions oily and sensitive skin buyers search for, such as barrier repair and what to avoid, each linked to the matching product.

2

Lifecycle

A check-in journey at weeks two, four and eight. Skincare results take time, so each message answers what to expect and when to adjust.

3

Reviews

Ask for reviews with skin type and routine length, and feature them on product pages so a sensitive-skin buyer sees someone like them.
