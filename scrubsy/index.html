A growth plan for Scrubsy

A plan to move a one-surface cleaner buyer to a full home-care basket and keep them refilling.

The starting point

Public fact: Describes itself as a performance-led home-care brand, with foam cleaners for kitchens, bathrooms and footwear (F4 Fund profile). Source

What I would test

1

Lifecycle

A surface ladder. A kitchen cleaner buyer gets a bathroom then footwear suggestion about two weeks after delivery, based on what they bought.

2

SEO

How-to pages for specific jobs, such as cleaning white sneakers or a greasy hob, each ending in the right foam cleaner.

3

Community

Before-and-after clips from customers. The best ones go on product pages and into a monthly leaderboard with product rewards.
