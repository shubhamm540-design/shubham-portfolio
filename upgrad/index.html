A growth plan for upGrad

A plan to win back learners who stopped after counselling, and turn finished learners into the next programme and referrals.

Email ShubhamLinkedIn

By Shubham Mishra, growth and lifecycle marketer. Built from public information only.

The starting point

Public fact: Reports a 19% rise in consumer learner enrolments in FY25, on gross revenue of ₹1,943 Cr (Entrackr). Source

What I would test

1

Lifecycle

Re-engage people who attended a counselling call but did not enrol, with a journey built around the goal they named, such as a career switch or a promotion.

2

Cross-sell

After a course, suggest the next step: a second programme, or internships and jobs. upGrad has said it is closing acquisitions of Internshala and Unacademy (press release, Aug 2026).

3

Referrals

An alumni referral programme with a simple leaderboard, so finished learners bring in the next batch.

How I would measure it

North star: Counselling-to-enrolment rateSecond-programme rate
