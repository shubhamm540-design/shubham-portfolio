A growth plan for Bla Bli Blu

A plan to turn first-time fragrance buyers, many from marketplaces, into customers the brand can reach again for gift sets, full sizes and the next scent.

Email ShubhamLinkedIn

By Shubham Mishra, growth and lifecycle marketer. Built from public information only.

The starting point

Public fact: Reports a ₹250 Cr annual run-rate and EBITDA-positive within about a year of its July 2025 launch (PTI, Oct 2026). An earlier D2C Insider report put about 70% of sales on marketplaces. Source

What I would test

1

Lifecycle

A pack QR leads to a "find your scent" quiz that enrols marketplace and quick-commerce buyers in WhatsApp and email. A journey follows: 30 ml gift set, then full size, then the next scent.

2

Reorder

Reminders timed to bottle size and the scent family the customer picked, sent by WhatsApp and push before the bottle runs out.

3

Community

A scent-of-the-week review loop. Customers rate and describe scents, and the top reviews feed product pages and the next gift set.

How I would measure it

North star: Repeat rate by channelGift set to full size conversion
