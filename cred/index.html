What I would test

1

Gamification

Streaks for paying on time earn bonus coins. I test whether streaks lift on-time payment and monthly opens.

2

Lifecycle

Coin balance and expiry nudges that point to the reward each member is most likely to want.

3

Referral

Reward members who bring a creditworthy friend, with a monthly leaderboard for top referrers.
