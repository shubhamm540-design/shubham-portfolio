My take

Key Findings & Opportunities

  1. Publish batch-level quality proof for hero SKUs and a plain response to the 2026 lab-test coverage.
  2. Fix mobile speed on countrydelight.in: it is mostly an app-download page (57 download buttons vs 18 shop buttons).
  3. Test quick commerce and subscription as separate offers, each with its own price rule.
  4. Give protein and ghee launches their own proof pages, not only celebrity ads.
  5. Prepare a clean public data story for IPO readers: one revenue definition, sourced and dated.

Sources: IMARC 2025 · IMARC 2024 report · Expert Market Research

  • IMARC puts the 2025 market at ₹12,730 Bn with liquid milk at 46.97% (IMARC). An earlier IMARC report has ₹18.97 tn for 2024 (Research and Markets). Same publisher, different scope.
  • Expert Market Research sees online as the fastest channel, at a 20.2% CAGR for 2025–2034 (EMR).
  • Mordor Intelligence has the market at $31.07B in 2025 and notes that Swiggy Instamart took 12% of Bengaluru home-delivered milk in three months after a deal with the state milk federation (Mordor).
  • Delhi dairy is ₹886.8 Bn in 2025, with loose-milk vendors still pricing below branded players (IMARC Delhi).
Why it worksMilk is a daily purchase, adulteration worries are real, and cold chain and apps make a branded doorstep offer possible.
Why it is hardLoose-milk vendors, cooperatives (Amul, Mother Dairy) and quick commerce all sell the same litre at lower friction.
Where Country Delight can winWhere freshness, a fixed time and proof of quality matter more than price per litre.
My take

The market is enormous, but the organised, online, subscription slice is small. The prize is to turn daily milk buyers into a full-basket customer without losing the morning habit.

Key Findings & Opportunities

  1. Quote one market definition (liquid milk, branded, home delivery) with its source.
  2. Track share of home-delivered milk in the top 10 cities.
  3. Monitor quick-commerce milk pricing weekly.
